Ask before you sign

  1. Can my caterer be anyone licensed, or only from your list?
  2. What is the outside-caterer fee, in dollars, for my guest count?
  3. What kitchen access does my caterer get (ovens, fridge, sinks)?
  4. Does my caterer need insurance naming the venue?
  5. Can we bring alcohol? Is a TABC bartender or security required?
  6. What time must cleanup end, and who removes trash?
