Quinceañera catering questions

Can family cook the food for a quinceañera?

Usually not at commercial venues, because of liability. Look for venues marked "Self-catering OK". Barns and some open-policy halls are the most likely to allow it; most others require a licensed caterer.

Do I need a TABC bartender in Texas?

Many DFW venues require a TABC-certified bartender if you bring alcohol, and some also require a paid security officer. Each listing notes the rule when the venue publishes it.

How many guests should I plan for?

Most DFW quinceañeras we see range from 150 to 300 guests. Use the guest filter to see only venues that fit.

Can we bring the cake and dessert table?

Many venues that restrict main catering still allow an outside cake from a licensed bakery. Ask the venue, and get it in writing.
